# Pokédex

A private family Pokédex for real Pokémon trading cards. Open it in a browser, add it to the home screen, and fill it in card by card.

- Every Pokémon from Generation 1 to 9 (1,025), browsable by generation, type, caught, missing and wish list.
- Add a card by typing the number from its bottom corner (like `025/198`) or the Pokémon's name, then tap the exact card.
- The real card picture is shown in the Pokédex, with levels, XP, rarity reveals, trophies and albums.
- The collection is saved on the device only. Use **Albums → Save a backup file** now and then.

Data: Pokémon details and artwork from [PokéAPI](https://pokeapi.co); card pictures and data from the [Pokémon TCG API](https://pokemontcg.io).
